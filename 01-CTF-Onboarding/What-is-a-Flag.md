What is a Flag in Capture The Flag (CTF)?
In cybersecurity CTF competitions, a flag is a specific, proof-of-concept text string that acts as the "reward" or proof that you have successfully solved a challenge.

When you reverse engineer a binary, exploit a vulnerability, crack a cipher, or inspect hidden files, your ultimate goal is to uncover this secret string and submit it to the platform to earn points.

🔍 Flag Formats & Structure
Flags typically follow a standardized syntax so that participants and scoring systems can easily identify them. A flag usually consists of three parts:

Prefix: Identifies the competition or platform hosting the challenge.

Wrapper Braces: {} surrounding the unique string.

Secret Payload: The unique characters, words, or hashes derived by solving the challenge.

Example Formats:
**picoCTF{1n_7h3_kk3y_of_f83f250a}**

**CTF{3xp1017_succ3ssfu1_1337}**

**flag{ba3e64_d3c0d3d_c0rr3c71y}**
